## 1. Abstract — the gate principle
A condition has to be met before the next one opens. Answer the block, reveal the panel. Pass the gate, see the whole. Lean is the strictest version: a proof assistant that will not open the next step until the previous one is checked.

## 2. Interactive gate
Twelve squares, locked. Each holds a question. Answer 1 through 12 in order (any nonempty answer passes). Out-of-order = "gate failed". 12/12 reveals the whole field.

1. If you were a medieval knight, what modern home appliance is your primary weapon?
2. You can instantly master any instrument, but only inside crowded elevators. Deal?
3. If animals had office jobs, which species is replying at 2:00 AM?
4. If your pet could leave a 1-star review, main complaint?
5. Is cereal technically a cold soup?
6. Absolute worst room to suddenly encounter a live lobster?
7. If you could outlaw one mild inconvenience forever, what gets banned?
8. Is a hot dog a sandwich, taco, or its own chaotic category?
9. Should AI be a consultative partner rather than a sovereign?
10. Is fluent language the same as consciousness?
11. Does an always-agreeable AI make real friction harder to bear?
12. What makes a shared truth trustworthy: provenance, peer review, or falsifiability?

## 3. Month map — full-year thesis
- **Month 01 — Agency, Governance & Power.** AI as consultative partner, not sovereign. Legitimacy from lived consequence, consent, inspectable reasoning. Safeguards: reversibility, right to fork, sunset clauses, exit rights, federated redundancy.
- **Month 02 — Consciousness, Perception & Sentience.** Intelligence ≠ consciousness. LLM fluency triggers agency projection. Simulation vs. embodied qualia, vulnerability, mortality.
- **Month 03 — Human Relationships & Emotional Intelligence.** Always-compliant AI may flatten tolerance for friction, grief, touch, forgiveness, conflict. Technology as scaffold, not destination.
- **Month 04 — Truth, Epistemology & Information Commons.** Provenance, open peer review, falsifiability, epistemic humility. Risks: synthetic flooding, engagement curation, personalized search.
