1 · Abstract — the gate principle

A condition has to be met before the next one opens. Answer the block, reveal the panel. Pass the gate, see the whole. Lean is the strictest version: a proof assistant that will not open the next step until the previous one is checked.

2 · Interactive gate

Twelve squares, locked. Each holds a question. Answer them in order, 1 through 12. Any honest, nonempty answer passes.

3 · Month map — full-year thesis

Month 01 — Agency, Governance & Power. AI as a consultative partner, not a sovereign. Legitimacy from lived consequence, consent, and inspectable reasoning. Safeguards: reversibility, right to fork, sunset clauses, exit rights, federated redundancy.

Month 02 — Consciousness, Perception & Sentience. Intelligence ≠ consciousness. LLM fluency triggers agency projection. Simulation vs. embodied qualia, vulnerability, mortality.

Month 03 — Human Relationships & Emotional Intelligence. Always-compliant AI may flatten our tolerance for friction, grief, touch, forgiveness, conflict. Technology as scaffold, not destination.

Month 04 — Truth, Epistemology & Information Commons. Provenance, open peer review, falsifiability, epistemic humility. Risks: synthetic flooding, engagement curation, personalized search.

Month 05 — Freedom, Dissent & Civil Rights. (continued)
